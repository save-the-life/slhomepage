First aid,
learned as a quiz.

Health Hero teaches first aid and everyday health knowledge through a stage-based quiz game. It runs inside the Toss app, and every answer — right or wrong — comes with an explanation.

Live in Apps in Toss
How to play

Open the Toss app and search for 헬스히어로.

No install, no separate account — it runs inside Toss.

What it does

One question, one piece of knowledge that stays.

Progression
Stage map

Unlock stages along a map and collect EXP and stars for every clear.

Content
Real-situation questions

Multiple-choice questions on mental health and stress, wounds, bleeding, fractures and other real situations.

Learning
Explanation on every answer

Right or wrong, the explanation appears immediately — so every question leaves something behind.

Pacing
Lives & items

Hearts are spent as you play and recharge over time. Use 50/50, hint, double-reward and reveal-answer items.

Growth
Levels & stars

Collect EXP to level up and build a star-point balance as you go.

Why it exists
Four minutes

In a cardiac arrest, brain damage begins in about four minutes. The person nearby is the one who decides how those minutes go.
