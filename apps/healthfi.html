What it does

Four things, every day.

Smartwatch measurement

Heart rate, blood pressure and single-lead ECG through a paired wearable — results are logged automatically.

Health-to-Earn rewards

Every measurement earns points, and daily and weekly bonuses keep the habit going.

Guardian alerts

When an abnormal reading is detected, family members are notified automatically by Telegram, SMS or KakaoTalk.

History & trends

Every reading is kept by date, so both the user and their family can see how things are changing at a glance.

Nobody should have to manage their health alone. HealthFi is built so a second person always knows.
