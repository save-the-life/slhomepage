What it does

Six loops that keep players coming back.

Core loop
Dice board

Move around a board where every tile holds points, an item or a mini-game. Auto-play is supported.

Mini-games
Roulette & Star Jackpot

A roulette event and a Star Jackpot slot. After a win, choose between doubling down or taking the payout.

Retention
Check-ins & missions

Weekly check-ins with a day-7 jackpot, one-off social missions on X, Telegram and Medium, plus USDT missions.

Competition
Season rankings

Players are ranked by star points each season, and the top of the table receives $SL tokens and tier passes.

Collection
Items & NFTs

Buy and hold ranking-booster items and NFT collections from the in-game shop.

Payout
Wallet & claim

Check $SL and USDT balances, claim rewards, and keep a full record of everything earned.
