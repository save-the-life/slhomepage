And when it happens with no one watching — an elderly parent at dawn, an empty living room in the afternoon — those four minutes are usually empty.

Golden time is decided before the ambulance arrives. So we build the network outside the hospital.

[09]Research & Validation

We publish our model cards.

A 4-year joint study with Gachon University and Seoul National University Hospital. 204,000+ ECG segments. AUROC 0.88 (clinical research). KFDA SaMD certification in progress.

Until certification, we say only "screening support" — never "diagnosis." That discipline is our brand.

Model Cards Publications KFDA SaMD Roadmap

SL Watch's wellness features are not a medical device and cannot determine the presence of disease. If an abnormal signal is detected, consult a licensed medical professional. Medical-device functions will be offered after KFDA clearance.
